07Who

THE OPERATOR

Helmet on, visor down, purple Sur-Ron underneath. My face has never been in a single video and it's not starting now.

Started in March 2025 with an unboxing video and a camera that honestly wasn't much good. 223 videos later there's a $10,000 Sur-Ron built from a bare frame, a 72V pack pushing 19,000 watts, a dirt track I cut myself, and a ride through Texas that ended in both crashes and cops. Somehow that adds up to 1.36 million views.

I ride everything I talk about. If a bike's slow, I say it's slow. If something breaks mid-review, that stays in too, I'm not cutting around it. That's basically the only reason anyone trusts what I say about a product.
