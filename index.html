// 07 — WHO

THE OPERATOR

Helmet on, visor down, purple Sur-Ron underneath. That's the whole brand — and it's deliberate. Nobody's here for my face.

I started in March 2025 with an unboxing and a bad camera. Since then it's been 223 videos: a $10,000 Sur-Ron built from the frame up, a 72V pack pushing 19,000 watts, a $5,000 street build, a secret dirt track cut by hand, and a Texas mega-ride that ended with crashes and cops. Along the way, 1.36 million views.

The channel works because I ride the things I talk about. If a bike is slow, it gets called slow. If a part fails, the failure makes the cut. That's the only reason anyone trusts the review — and it's the reason a brand should want to be on it.
