07Who

THE OPERATOR

Helmet on, visor down, purple Sur-Ron underneath. I have never shown my face on the channel and I am not going to.

I started in March 2025 with an unboxing and a bad camera. Since then I have posted 223 videos. A $10,000 Sur-Ron built up from a bare frame, a 72V pack pushing 19,000 watts, a dirt track I cut by hand, and a Texas ride ending in crashes and police. 1.36 million views so far.

I ride everything I talk about. If a bike is slow I say so. If a part breaks, the failure stays in the video. People trust the reviews for exactly one reason, and brands should want to be on the right side of it.
